### Shewbread 陈设饼

A free bilingual Bible reader. Chinese and English versions sit side by side, verse by verse, and any verse opens down to the Greek and Hebrew, word by word, with full lexicons and cross-references.

- Read: [read.shewbread.net](https://read.shewbread.net)
- About: [shewbread.net](https://shewbread.net)
- Contact, permissions and copyright: contact@shewbread.org

We use this account to take part in the open projects our texts and tools come from: reporting errata, discussing editions, and sharing fixes back.
